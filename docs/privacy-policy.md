# Mirror — Privacy Policy

*Draft for legal review. Last updated: [DATE]*

Mirror Technologies FZ-LLC ("Mirror", "we") operates the Mirror: Meet on Purpose app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 1. Who can use Mirror
Mirror is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; accounts found to belong to minors are deleted.

## 2. What we collect
**Account:** your email address (used to sign in; never shown on your profile).
**Profile:** first name, date of birth (only your age is shown), gender, who you want to meet, city, photos, relationship intention, and optional details you choose to add — relationship status, children, lifestyle, hobbies, faith, height, education, profession and cultural background.
**Preferences:** your description of your type, the traits we derive from it, flexibility and deal-breakers, and what matters most to you.
**Activity:** introductions you were shown, your choices (interested / not for me and optional reasons), matches, messages, feedback, reports and blocks.
**Verification:** a selfie and an image of a government ID. These are stored privately, viewed only by our trust & safety team (or a verification provider acting for us), and used solely to confirm you are a real adult who matches your photos. We keep only the verified / not verified result on your profile.
**Purchases:** subscription and Boost status from Apple / Google via RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.
**Device:** push-notification token, app version and crash diagnostics (via Sentry, without personal profile content).
**Location:** your city, from your device (with permission) or entered manually. We do not store your precise location.

## 3. Sensitive information
Faith, cultural or ethnic background and your dating preferences may reveal sensitive information. Providing them is optional. For each, you choose whether it is shown on your profile and whether it is used for matching. You can change these at any time in Settings → Privacy.

## 4. How we use your data
To create your account and profile; to curate introductions and explain compatibility; to enable matching and messaging; to keep the community safe (verification, reports, pattern notices, moderation); to process purchases; to send notifications you've enabled; to fix bugs and improve the service. We do **not** sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

## 5. What other members see
Only your profile card: first name, age, city, photos and the details you choose to show. Never your email, exact date of birth, ID, verification selfie, or hidden fields.

## 6. Service providers
Supabase (database, storage, authentication — hosting region per our project), Apple and Google (app distribution, payments), RevenueCat (subscription management), Expo (push notification delivery), Sentry (crash reporting), Google Gemini (to interpret the "describe your type" text you submit; not used to train their models under our API terms), and our email provider. Each processes data only to provide its service to us.

## 7. Retention
We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, matches, messages and verification files are permanently deleted. Reports you made or that concern you may be retained in limited form for up to [12] months where needed for safety or legal reasons.

## 8. Your rights
You can access, correct, download or delete your data at any time (Settings → Privacy → Download data / Delete account) or by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Depending on where you live (including the UK GDPR and UAE PDPL), you may also object to or restrict certain processing and complain to your data protection authority.

## 9. Security
Data is encrypted in transit. Access to member data is restricted by database security rules; verification images are in private storage accessible only to authorised reviewers.

## 10. International transfers
Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## 11. Changes
We will notify you in the app of material changes to this policy.
