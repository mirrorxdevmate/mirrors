## Description (both stores)
Mirror is dating for people who mean it.

Instead of endless swiping, you get a small set of curated introductions each day — people who fit the type you describe in your own words, the relationship you want, and what matters most to you.

WHY YOU TWO?
Every introduction comes with a plain-language explanation of the fit: shared values, relationship goals, lifestyle — and anything worth knowing before you say hello. Mirror explains the fit. You decide.

DESCRIBE YOUR TYPE
Tell Mirror who catches your attention, in your own words. Mirror turns it into preferences you can edit, and you choose what is flexible and what is a deal-breaker.

EVERYONE IS VERIFIED
Every member completes a photo check and an ID check before they can meet anyone. Your ID is never shown on your profile.

SAFETY, BUILT IN
Report and block in two taps. Trust signals on every profile. Pattern notices when someone's behaviour doesn't match their stated intentions. Our team reviews every report.

YOUR PATTERN
See what your choices say about what you're really drawn to — privately, and only for you.

MIRROR+ AND PREMIUM (optional subscriptions)
• Mirror+: faster refresh, larger curated batches, Advanced Type Controls, Global Discovery, See Who's Interested, Rewind
• Premium: everything in Mirror+, plus deeper "Why you two?", Your Pattern+, Travel Mode and Incognito Mode
